The business date is the financial clock used by the application. Advance Day locks that date, checks for draft and submitted journals, snapshots leaf-account balances, and moves to a selected later date in one database transaction. Interest accrual runs separately; this action is not a complete end-of-day workflow. Next, I will explain three design decisions behind the implementation.

Key design decisions

Three decisions keep the accounting model explicit

Running balances support fast reads. Rebuilds and clear boundaries keep the design maintainable.

Decision 01

Store balance_after on every entry

Why Statements and audit reads stay fast without replaying history.
Trade-off Corrections need a rebuild path for the affected accounts.
Decision 02

Read the latest posted running balance

Why Current balances use the latest posted entry rather than replaying every journal.
Trade-off Backdated entries require running balances to be recalculated in order.
Decision 03 Out of scope

Keep external settlement out of scope

Why No interbank payment network is connected to this prototype.
Trade-off Loan events use selected internal cash, bank or clearing accounts.

The principle: keep balances traceable and be explicit about what the system does not do.

Challenges and fixes

Three accounting risks shaped the implementation

Each risk led to a specific control, rather than another manual workaround.

Challenge

Running balances drift after corrections

A stored running balance can disagree with the ledger.

Fix

Rebuild from posted entries

Store balance_after, reverse mistakes and rebuild posted entries in effective-date order.

Challenge

Customer detail would bloat the chart

Thousands of customer accounts would swamp the chart of accounts.

Fix

Control account plus subledger

Customer balances sit behind Customer Control; postings to control accounts are blocked.

Challenge

Day-end could close a half-finished day

A manual checklist can miss a draft or submitted journal.

Fix

One atomic date change

Lock the date, block unposted journals, snapshot leaf balances and advance atomically.
